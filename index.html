Zone 2 target is 30–45 min. Cadence 95–105 rpm at low resistance — tendon and patellofemoral force track pedal force, so spin light rather than grind. Seated only while the tendon is symptomatic. If the talk test fails, that ride was Zone 3 and it competes with your lifting.
Isometrics / home
5 × 45 s at roughly 70% effort, 2 min rest. Long enough to be a real stimulus, and often analgesic for 30–45 min afterwards — useful immediately before a squat session. The pain-relief evidence is mixed and short-lived; treat it as a tool, not a cure. Next-morning response is read automatically from tomorrow's check.

Weekly is enough. Single readings are noisy; the trend over a month is the signal — for training load and for blood pressure.
